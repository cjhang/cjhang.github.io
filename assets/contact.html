I am currently a postdoc at the Research School of Astronomy and Astrophysics (RSAA) at the Australian National University, working with Melanie Kaasinen. Before this, I was a postdoctoral fellow at

Max Planck Institute for Extraterrestrial Physics (MPE), where I worked with Linda Tacconi and Natascha M. Förster Schreiber. I obtained my PhD at the European Southern Observatory (ESO), under the supervision of Rob Ivison. My research focuses primarily on distant, dusty star-forming galaxies, but I am interested in all aspects of galaxy formation and evolution. Outside research, I enjoy photography, hiking, cycling, cooking, and dreaming.
